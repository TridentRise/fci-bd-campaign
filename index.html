Working Rules (how this campaign runs)

  1. Cold email gets no replies. Emails create the paper trail; events and warm paths are the connection strategy.
  2. Never reference a target's breaches or fines in outreach. Incidents inform the plan internally; outreach stays forward-looking (general BYOD-field framing).
  3. Verify every contact is currently in role before outreach — LinkedIn snippets show past roles as present (the Vanderpoel lesson).
  4. Plans are drafts until Mike's history and contacts are layered in — strategize together before sending.
  5. Standard sales flow: Call 1 (Mike, 30-min intro/fit) → questionnaire → Call 2 (deep dive/proposal). Entry ask = 30-minute Security Assessment / Gap Analysis.

The Four Buying Models (independent-field / BYOD segment)

How a firm buys determines how we grade and work the account. Historically many BDs said field security wasn't their responsibility; regulators are slowly changing that. The ladder:

# Model What the firm does What it means for FCI Verdict
1 Endorse Recommends/suggests FCI to its field; reps buy one-by-one Slow, retail-style motion Acceptable only as a true partnership (active promotion, access, co-marketing)
2 Anchor Enterprise buys the visibility/inventory layer; promotes field adoption of the rest Enterprise base + field expansion Strong entry — beachhead that grows
3 Split Enterprise buys core controls for every rep; field tops up for full compliance Large enterprise deal + expansion Primary target model
4 Full Enterprise Buys everything for everyone The unicorn Ideal; most realistic in Lane 2 (career carriers) and Lane 3 (employee-device firms)

Every Lane 1 account plan should carry a "realistic model today → path upward" note. Regulatory pressure is the ratchet that moves firms up.

The Internal Champion Concept (the "Dave Yeager" Guideline)

The archetype: FCI's champion at Guardian was Dave Yeager — Head of Agency Cybersecurity & Governance (retired May 2022 after 34 years; successor in the seat: Leon Charles). He sat between cybersecurity and the field, with governance accountability — created specifically because regulators forced the question. He was the champion by a mile — but the exact role is a guideline, not a title to match. Every large firm structures the field differently; the search target is: whoever is accountable for cybersecurity actually reaching the field force.

The champion is usually NOT the ultimate decision maker. Working buying-committee model: economic buyer = CCO/CRO (owns the exam consequence) · champion = the Yeager-equivalent (owns the daily pain) · influencer = CISO/CTO.

LinkedIn Follower Mining

Followers of the company page already raised a hand. Workflow, monthly:

  1. Export the company-page follower list (page-admin view / Sales Navigator cross-reference)
  2. Classify — every follower scored against the four lanes: firm, role, seniority, champion-archetype match (AI-assisted; the list comes back tiered)
  3. Tier — A: matches a target account or connector archetype (goes into an account plan or the referral campaign) · B: right firm, wrong role (map to the right colleague) · C: no fit
  4. Engage — Tier A gets deliberate motions: content engagement, warm connect, event invitations — never a cold pitch to a follower

First run requires the follower export — then classification and tiering are same-day.

Most broker-dealer networks run on a BYOD field model — advisors working from their own devices, on their own networks, in offices no central IT team manages. Controls get defined at the home office; proving they actually reach every device is another matter.

FCI was built for exactly that environment. We work exclusively with financial services firms — 30+ years, 40,000+ endpoints under management — enforcing controls on field and BYOD devices without requiring IT presence or asking advisors to change how they work. One example: every device is validated before it connects, so an unmanaged or compromised endpoint never becomes the incident an office has to explain.

The result: field cybersecurity posture becomes documentation you pull, not evidence chased office by office.
